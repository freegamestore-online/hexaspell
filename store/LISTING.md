# HexaSpell — store listing

**Title:** HexaSpell

**Category:** Puzzle
**Type:** Standalone (no backend for play; daily results are saved in the browser and can be posted to the leaderboard)

**One-liner:** Build words from a honeycomb of seven letters. A daily puzzle, practice mode, and free hints. No ads, no tracking.

## Description

HexaSpell is a word game on a honeycomb of seven letters. Every word needs at least four letters and must use the centre letter. Find as many as you can. A word that uses all seven letters is a pangram, and it earns a bonus and a celebration.

- A new daily puzzle every UTC day, the same for everyone. Finish today to post your score to the leaderboard.
- Practice mode, with fresh random puzzles whenever you like.
- Ranks from Beginner to Genius as your score grows.
- Free hints: a grid of answers by first letter and length, the two-letter starts, and yesterday's answers.
- Tap the honeycomb or type on a keyboard. Works in light and dark themes, and respects reduced motion.
- Works offline once loaded. Your progress is saved in your browser.
- Free forever, MIT-licensed, with no ads, accounts or tracking.
